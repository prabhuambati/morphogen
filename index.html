A living image experiment

See your image come apart—and come back changed.

Choose an example or upload a photo. Morphogen turns its colors into cell-sized image patches, then lets a cellular automaton dissolve and rebuild the picture.

What is a cellular automaton?

A cellular automaton is a grid of cells that changes in steps according to simple local rules. In Morphogen, each living cell carries a patch of the source image. Neighboring cells influence births and deaths while a structural skeleton preserves important contours, so the picture evolves without losing all of its identity.

How to make this effect

Upload a PNG, JPG, or WebP, choose a grid and structure setting, then press Morph. Pause at any point to explore the evolving image, or use Reconstruct to watch it rebuild. Record a video or export a watermarked PNG to share your result.
